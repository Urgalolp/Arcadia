The Null is an era known to have existed in between 1 B.C. and 1 A.C., in which many believe that the figure known as Jesus Christ has lived.

There is no direct information regarding The Null, hence its name, despite the fact that historians have been able to catalog information prior to this era and have been able to piece together what they believe to be events within this era. Unfortunately, there is no exact known duration for this period, with many believing it lasted at least 30 years.

Though many scholars, intellectually or spiritually, disagree on precisely what has occurred within this period, the one thing they do agree on is that something of great consequence has happened during The Null. Proof of that is the concept of splicing… an artifact common in the modern age, yet that did not exist prior to this era.
