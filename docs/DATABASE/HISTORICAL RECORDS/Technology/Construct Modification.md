### Integration

Constructs of technological origin, whether developed through conventional engineering or through the combination of technology and magic, have grown considerably more sophisticated over time. Early constructs were generally limited by the materials, manufacturing techniques, and magical processes available at the time of their creation, resulting in comparatively rigid designs with limited potential for modification.

Advancements in construct technology have gradually removed many of these restrictions. Modern constructs can be designed with greater modularity, allowing weapons, tools, armor, and other equipment to be incorporated directly into their bodies. In many cases, these integrations can even be performed after the construct itself has been completed, granting considerable freedom for later customization. Among all known examples, the Warforged are perhaps the most prominent demonstration of this technology.
### Modification

Creatures with the **Construct** type are able to modify themselves by integrating weapons, tools, armor, and other forms of gear within their bodies. Doing so normally requires **1 hour of work** for each integration.

A creature that benefits from this capability has a number of **modification slots** equal to its Constitution modifier. Constructs that are not technological in nature cannot be modified in this manner, while constructs that are **technologically adjacent** have half as many modification slots.
