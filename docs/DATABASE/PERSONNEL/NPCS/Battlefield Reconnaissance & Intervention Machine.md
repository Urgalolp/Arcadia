**Designation:** Battlefield Reconnaissance & Intervention Machine  
**Race:** Warforged  
**Estimated Creation:** 1000–1100 A.C.
**Occupation:** Unknown
**Affiliations:** Itself

B.R.I.M. is a 7'2", 520 lbs individual whose only known designation is its acronym, **Battlefield Reconnaissance & Intervention Machine**. Even this designation is not regarded by B.R.I.M. as a proper name, and the entity demonstrates little attachment to the concept of names in general. It possesses no discernible concept of gender and, more significantly, appears to possess little understanding of individuality. B.R.I.M. does not particularly regard itself as a singular being and shows little concern for whether it is perceived as one.

B.R.I.M. is a Warforged believed to have been created sometime between 1000 and 1100 A.C. The circumstances of its creation are unknown. Neither its original creator nor its place of manufacture can be reliably identified, largely due to the extensive modifications B.R.I.M. has made to its primary body over the centuries.

It remains unclear whether B.R.I.M. has continuously operated from the same original body, replacing and reconstructing individual components to the point that its original construction is no longer recognizable, or whether it has transferred its core into entirely new bodies throughout its existence. Both possibilities are considered plausible, and B.R.I.M. itself has provided little information that would resolve the matter.

B.R.I.M. does not formally support any nation, government, or major organization, nor is it known to receive support from one. It generally avoids actions that would place its continued existence at significant risk, although it is willing to accept such risks when the expected benefit outweighs the potential consequences. Considerable effort has therefore been made to avoid attracting the sustained attention of any particular government or intelligence agency. Most of B.R.I.M.'s known dealings occur within the criminal underworld and other unofficial networks.

Only a limited number of agencies are known to be aware of B.R.I.M.'s existence. Those that are generally avoid provoking it without sufficient reason. On its own, B.R.I.M. is not considered an extraordinary threat by modern standards. The concern lies instead in what it has been allowed to accomplish over the considerable length of time it has remained active.

B.R.I.M. has demonstrated the ability to construct artificial lifeforms which function as extensions of itself, as well as develop and manufacture weaponry capable of inflicting catastrophic damage upon critical assets. Consequently, eliminating B.R.I.M. is considered considerably more dangerous than its individual capabilities would initially suggest.

For this reason, agencies possessing knowledge of B.R.I.M. generally adopt a policy of deliberate non-interference. So long as it remains uninvolved in matters of significant concern, authorities have little incentive to expend the personnel and resources necessary to remove it. B.R.I.M., in turn, appears content to maintain this arrangement.
