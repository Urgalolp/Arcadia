Standing 5’6”, and weighing about 130 lbs, Althir is a seemingly human male of Russian descendancy, bearing a skin of pale coloration, which hard to tell apart from snow, a beige hair, which goes beyond his shoulders, and a set of gray eyes inlaid on dark sclera. From a pure visual standpoint, one would find it hard to describe him as much more than delicate.

By all accounts, Althir is a skinny individual, lacking the musculature of a military soldier. Paradoxically, Althir displays feats of athleticism akin to a healthy and trained individual, contradicting his appearance. As with most living creatures with sufficient sentience to be deemed ‘humans’, it is inferred that he possesses spliced genes, though the extent of such is unknown, and no circumstance has led to a perceivable effect, drawback, or benefit afflicting him that could be traced to any specific set of genes. 

Althir is known to be part of the Russian military to some extent, but he does not divulge any information about it. Althir is also particularly old, overly so, with his exact origins being unclear.
