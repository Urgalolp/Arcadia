**Nationality:** Dutch  
**Callsign:** Piet  
**Race:** Unknown  
**Age:** Unknown  
**Occupation:** Smuggler / Black-Market Supplier  
**Affiliations:** Independent; Private Smuggling Crew

A Dutch smuggler of rather limited reputation. Piet’s skills as a smuggler are not incredible; there are others in the trade with considerably more experience, greater resources, and far more reliable networks. She operates with a tight-knit crew, frequently relies on third parties to handle tasks beyond her capabilities, and has a tendency to find herself in troublesome situations. Her greatest advantage, perhaps, is simply her availability.

Piet deals in transport, goods, and information, and can usually acquire almost anything that is not readily available through legitimate means. She is willing to take on most requests when contacted, though difficult or particularly valuable jobs often come at a premium. Delivery can be slow, and dealing with Piet herself requires a certain tolerance for her antics, but she remains a dependable option for those seeking something that is neither particularly secret nor exceptionally high-end.

Standing at only 3'8" and weighing approximately 55 pounds, Piet is considerably smaller than the average humanoid. Her diminutive stature has done little to prevent her from establishing herself within the smuggling trade, though it certainly contributes to the peculiar impression she tends to leave on those who meet her.
