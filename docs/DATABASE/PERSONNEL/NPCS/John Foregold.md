John Foregold. Australian citizen, Austrian officer, classic British enthusiast. A tall roach man whose competence is only second to his quality as a pushover and his love for sweets of all kinds. For all intents and purposes, John is a non-threat, as he is not a fighter and is also not a very social individual. When it comes to data collection, however, that changes.

Though an easy person to handle, John is highly capable of acquiring, digesting, and compiling information of all kinds, whether it be digitally or physically, through books, servers, datapads, or even from the environment. His greatest quality is his observational skills and absurd intellect, which seems to be geared entirely at processing information like a computer and piling it together in an understandable manner.
