**Nationality:** Australian
**Callsign:** Roach
**Race:** Presumably Roachkin
**Age:** 18
**Occupation:** Intelligence and Information Specialist
**Affiliations:** Austrian Military; Unfair Affairs;

**John Foregold** is a 6'7", 245 lbs Australian citizen serving as an Austrian officer, and an enthusiastic admirer of practically anything associated with classic British culture. He is a tall, roach-like individual whose competence is rivaled only by his tendency to be easily pushed around and his seemingly inexhaustible fondness for sweets of all kinds.

For most practical purposes, John is considered a non-threat. He is neither a fighter nor a particularly social individual, and possesses little inclination toward direct confrontation. Those qualities, however, are of little relevance when John is given access to information.

John possesses an exceptional aptitude for acquiring, processing, and compiling information. He is capable of gathering data through virtually any available medium, whether digitally or physically: books, servers, datapads, documents, recordings, or even direct observation of his surroundings. The medium itself appears to matter little to him.

His greatest assets are his observational ability and his extraordinary intellect. John's mind appears almost purpose-built for the processing of information, allowing him to identify relevant details, connect otherwise unrelated pieces of data, and organize large quantities of information into coherent and understandable records.

Given sufficient time and access to a source of information, John can produce remarkably comprehensive compilations of the material available to him. His capabilities make him considerably more valuable as an intelligence asset than his otherwise unassuming demeanor would suggest.

John is therefore rarely a dangerous individual in the conventional sense. His danger, if any, lies in what he knows, what he can discover, and how efficiently he can make sense of it.
