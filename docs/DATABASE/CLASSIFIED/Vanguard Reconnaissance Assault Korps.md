Despite its name, the USA’s VRAK is less of a unit designed for reconnaissance and head-on combat, and more of a unit designed for containment, retrieval, and elimination. It was originally created as a unit that would independently collect information on perceived threats and engage them at will with the intent of sabotaging, stalling, or eliminating them, but overtime the VRAK evolved into a more specialized force, leaving the roles of intelligence to more specialized units, and falling short of foreign forces when it comes to pure firepower.

Despite that, VRAK remained as one of the primary thorns in the side of independent agents, compensating in quantity of personnel what it lacks in quality of personnel, and retaining its versatility where other units of its kind fail to. It is perhaps the unit that prepares the most counter-measures when it comes to handling threats.
