**Established:** Unknown  
**Organization Type:** Special Operations Unit
**Presumed Origin:** Germany

The Krieg Wanton, commonly referred to as the Kriegs, are a highly specialized military organization believed to originate from Germany. Their personnel are outfitted with advanced, high-grade equipment and are reportedly subjected to extensive physical and biological preparation for combat. Kriegs are trained and conditioned to carry out their orders regardless of the danger involved, with little apparent consideration given to their own survival.

The result is a unit capable of producing extreme levels of violence wherever it is deployed. Among individuals familiar with the international underworld, the Kriegs have developed a reputation as a particularly dangerous organization, regarded as a threat to virtually anything placed in their path.

It is widely accepted within criminal and intelligence circles that Germany is responsible for the creation, maintenance, and deployment of the Kriegs. No conclusive evidence supporting this claim has been obtained, however. The German government has taken the unusual position of neither denying the existence of the organization nor acknowledging any connection to it. Official statements instead characterize the Kriegs as an independent entity, while German authorities are known to actively investigate their activities.

Among known special operations organizations, the Kriegs are uniquely associated with the deliberate use of overwhelming force against designated threats and targets. Their deployments reportedly carry few restrictions concerning collateral damage, resource expenditure, or personnel casualties, provided the assigned objective is achieved.

For this reason, the Kriegs are often described as a sledgehammer: not a unit intended for subtlety or efficiency, but an instrument deployed when a target is considered sufficiently dangerous that overwhelming force is deemed preferable to conventional methods.
