The standard body armor for police tactical units, this vest provides full-torso protection in the toughest flexible protective materials available. While wearing this armor, your AC is 16 + your dexterity modifier (a max of +2), and you have disadvantage on stealth checks. It has a weight of 16 lbs, costs 900 credits, and requires a strength score of 13 to be worn.
