A piece of equipment is integrated into the suit to provide additional benefits to its wearer. Choose any one gear. That gear is integrated into the base armor and can be used at any time, hands free. If the equipment is consumable, it has one ‘charge’ of its use, but can be refilled at 1/4 of the cost. This modification costs 2 times the price of the gear.
* **Restriction:** Only medium and heavy armors. This modification can be applied a maximum of three times.
