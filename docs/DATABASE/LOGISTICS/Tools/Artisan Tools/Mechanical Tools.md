Mechanical tools allow you to work with machinery, mechanical assemblies, engines, and other devices composed primarily of moving or interconnected physical components. They are used to assemble, disassemble, maintain, repair, and modify mechanical equipment, with particular emphasis on identifying how its components interact.

**Components.** Mechanical tools include wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers, sockets, hammers, hex keys, files, measuring tools, lubricants, and assorted fasteners and small replacement components.

**Investigation.** Your expertise allows you to identify mechanical faults, determine how a device operates, and recognize signs of tampering, wear, or deliberate sabotage.

**Perception.** You can identify subtle irregularities in mechanical devices, such as unusual wear, damaged components, loose fittings, or sounds indicating internal faults.

**Repair.** With access to your tools and appropriate replacement parts, you can restore damaged mechanical equipment. The time and materials required depend on the severity of the damage and complexity of the device.

**Tinkering.** Your knowledge allows you to modify mechanical devices, construct simple mechanisms, or adapt components from one machine for use in another.

Some activities can be done with more ease when using this tool, and some can only be done with this tool, such as the exceptions (and their DCs) below.

- Repair a simple mechanical device (10)
- Disassemble a mechanical device without damaging its components (10)
- Identify the source of a mechanical failure (10)
- Disable a mechanical device (15)
- Repair a vehicle component (15)
- Modify a mechanical device for a new purpose (15)
- Reconstruct a damaged mechanism from its components (20)
