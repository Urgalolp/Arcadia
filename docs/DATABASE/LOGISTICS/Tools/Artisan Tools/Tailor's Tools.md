Tailor's tools allow you to cut, measure, sew, repair, and alter fabrics and other textiles. A skilled tailor can produce clothing to precise measurements, repair damaged garments, identify flaws in construction, and modify existing clothing without compromising its appearance or function.

**Components.** Tailor's tools include needles, thread, measuring tape, fabric shears, pins, chalk, a thimble, and assorted fasteners.

**Investigation.** Your expertise allows you to identify unusual stitching, alterations, hidden compartments, or other irregularities in clothing and textiles.

**Perception.** You can notice details in garments that others might overlook, such as concealed pockets, recently repaired sections, or inconsistencies in fabric and stitching.

**Deception.** Your knowledge of clothing allows you to disguise the appearance of garments, alter uniforms, or create convincing changes in size, style, or condition.

**Repair.** With access to your tools and suitable materials, you can restore a damaged textile object, repairing tears, replacing fasteners, or reconstructing portions of fabric.

Some activities can be done with more ease when using this tool, and some can only be done with this tool, such as the exceptions (and their DCs) below.

- Repair damaged clothing (10)
- Alter clothing to fit a different individual (10)
- Conceal a pocket or compartment within clothing (15)
- Reconstruct damaged clothing from fragments (15)
- Replicate a garment from observation (15)
- Produce a convincing disguise from existing clothing (20)
