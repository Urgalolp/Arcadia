Attacks with weapons or ammunition with the armor piercing property are quite effective against armor, whether natural or otherwise. When a weapon or ammunition piece has the armor piercing trait, they ignore that much damage reduction when dealing damage. 

Ammunition of this type has an armor piercing value (AP) of 1 per die size, from an AP of 1 at d4, to an AP of 5 at d12. Armor Piercing from weapons always stacks with armor piercing from ammunition, to a maximum of 10.

Because you generally do not know the damage reduction of your target, you express it when rolling damage. If you deal, say, 15 points of damage to a creature with, say, an AP 2 weapon, you express it as "15 damage, AP 2."
