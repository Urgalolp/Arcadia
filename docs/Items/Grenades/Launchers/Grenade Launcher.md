A [[Grenades]] launcher costs 80000 credits, weighs 8 lbs, and has the properties “Two-Handed”, “Reload (5)” and “Loading”. It uses grenades as ammunition. A creature must use an action to load a single grenade into it, or a bonus action if they are proficient. A grenade launcher doubles the effective range, but not radius, of the grenade, and is able to prime it, allowing the wielder to choose whether the grenade will detonate on impact or at the start of the wielder’s next turn.

A grenade launcher can be used as a ranged firearm, and its wielder makes attack rolls as normal if the point the grenade is being launched at is a creature or object that can be targeted for an attack. A direct hit causes the target to automatically fail its save and deals a base damage of 1 bludgeoning damage. A miss grants the target advantage on the saving throw.
