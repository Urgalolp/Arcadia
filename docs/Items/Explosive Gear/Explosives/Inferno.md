These explosives create a large fireball when they explode, leaving behind a sticky substance. When activated, each creature within 10 feet of the explosive must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

A creature that succeed this save is ignited until the end of their next turn, and a creature that fails is ignited for one minute or until extinguished. An ignited creature cannot hide and takes 2d6 fire damage at the start of its every turn.
