Attacks with weapons or ammunition with the armor piercing property are quite effective against armor, whether natural or otherwise. Armor Piercing always stacks, to a maximum of 10. When a weapon or ammunition piece has the armor piercing trait, they ignore that much damage reduction when dealing damage.

Because you generally do not know the damage reduction of your target, you  express it when rolling damage. If you deal, say, 15 points of damage to a creature with, say, an armor piercing 2 weapon, you express it as "15 damage, armor piercing 2."

Armor piercing rounds come in three varieties: [[Semi-Piercing]], [[Piercing]], and [[Sabot]]
