Following similar principles as armor piercing [[Rounds]], these bullets carry far less velocity, often from being utilized in small rifle or pistol calibers. They are typically used in situations where a target is hid flimsy cover, such as sheet metal. Any modern firearm which uses bullets for its ammunition can fire semi-armor piercing bullets. A firearm which uses semi-armor piercing bullets gains the armor piercing (-1) property. If weapon already has armor piercing property, then that property increases by 1.

A box with 10 pieces of this Ammunition costs 12500 credits.
