Any modern firearm which uses bullets for its ammunition can fire hollow point [[Rounds]]. A firearm which uses hollow point rounds (also known as "dumdums") deals 1 additional point of damage per damage die of the weapon, as the bullets "mushroom" on impact with a soft target and expand, causing larger wound channels. However, hollow point rounds penetrate poorly and cannot strike through armor. A creature with natural or worn armor and an AC of 20 or higher has resistance to damage from hollow point rounds.  A creature with resistance to piercing damage takes no damage from hollow point rounds.

A box with 10 pieces of this Ammunition costs 10000 credits.
