Incendiary [[Rounds]] are bullets which contain a flammable compound, such as phosphorous or hydrogen gas. Upon impact, the compound ignites with a flash of heat that can ignite fires. Any modern firearm which uses bullets for its ammunition can fire incendiary bullets. Half the damage of firearm attacks made with incendiary bullets (rounded down) is converted to fire damage, while the remaining half is still piercing damage. A flammable object hit by an incendiary bullet ignites if it isn't being worn or carried. Incendiary bullets are useful for destroying electronics, fuel, and ammunition.

A box with 10 Incendiary rounds costs 10000 credits. If the ammunition pieces are also [[Armor Piercing]], this cost is instead added on top of the original cost for said rounds.
