Match grade [[Rounds]] are very well made, subjected to more rigorous than usual quality control, and designed for an extremely narrow tolerance and precise accuracy. Because they are so hard to make, they are expensive. Any modern firearm which uses bullets for its ammunition can fire match grade rounds. If you are using a firearm with a match grade barrel, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls with match grade bullets.

A box with 10 match grade rounds costs 15000 credits.
