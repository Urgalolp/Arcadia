On a hit, an explosive [[Rounds]] releases both extreme heat and concussive force, dealing an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage and 1d6 fire damage. Explosive rounds, contrary to what you might think, don't actually contain enough explosive power to have an appreciable area effect - there's only so much power you can pack into a pistol or rifle bullet. Explosive rounds can also be [[Armor Piercing]], which is often common as they are more effective if they can pierce the layer of armor.

A box with 10 pieces of this Ammunition costs 15000 credits. If the ammunition pieces are also [[Armor Piercing]], this cost is instead added on top of the original cost for said rounds.
