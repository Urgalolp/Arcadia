The suit of armor is reinforced with additional platings, greatly boosting its overall protection at the cost of weight. The AC granted by this armor is increased by 1. Every time this modification is applied, the armor’s weight increases by 5 lbs. This modification costs 100 credits, increased by 50 credits for each point of AC the armor already has. 
* **Restriction:** The armor cannot have a concealability DC, and must not have the **Ultralight** modification.
