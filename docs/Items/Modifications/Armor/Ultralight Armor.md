The suit of armor is modified with lighter alloys or fabrics of sparse, not dense, distribution, reducing its overall bulk and weight. The AC granted by this armor is reduced by 1. The weight of the armor is halved (round up), and if the armor has a Strength requirement it is reduced by 2. This modification costs 200 credits.
* **Restriction:** The armor must not have the **Additional Plating** modification.
