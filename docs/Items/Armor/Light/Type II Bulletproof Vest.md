Most bulletproof armor is made from ballistic paneling that chips away on impact, redirecting the force of a gunshot. Type II weighs 8 lbs and is designed for high velocity, small caliber weapons, such as relatively high-power pistols. This is the level of armor commonly seen among law enforcement and civilians. Most forms of this armor can be concealed under a shirt. While wearing this armor, your AC is 10 + your dexterity modifier, and you have a damage reduction of 4, reduced to 2 against melee attacks, and to 1 against explosives. If you are proficient with light armor, your AC increases by your proficiency bonus.

This set of armor costs 50000 credits.
