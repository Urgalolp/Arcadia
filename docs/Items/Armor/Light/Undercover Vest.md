Undercover Vest
Designed for discreet protection without sacrificing mobility, this vest is worn beneath ordinary clothing and uses a thin layer of protective material to provide substantially better protection than improvised or lightly armored garments. Its low-profile construction allows it to be worn beneath a shirt or jacket without immediately appearing to be body armor. While wearing this armor, your AC is 13 + your dexterity modifier. It has a weight of 3 lbs and costs 350 credits. It has a concealability DC of 18.
