Designed for deep undercover work in which it’s critical that the wearer not appear to be armed or armored, this garment consists of a t-shirt with a band of light protective material sewn in around the lower torso. While wearing this armor, your AC is 11 + your dexterity modifier. It has a weight of 2 lbs and costs 200 credits. It has a concealability DC of 24.
