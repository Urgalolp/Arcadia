Most bulletproof armor is made from ballistic paneling that chips away on impact, redirecting the force of a gunshot. Type IV weighs 18 lbs and is designed for protection against military grade rifles loaded with armor-piercing ammunition. This is the armor of choice for frontline soldiers expecting combat. Most militaries will outfit their soldiers with this level of armor, if they get the chance. While wearing this armor, your AC is 10 + your dexterity modifier (to a maximum of 2), and you have a damage reduction of 7, reduced to 4 against melee attacks, and to 2 against explosives.  If you are proficient with medium armor, your AC increases by your proficiency bonus.

This set of armor costs 250000 credits.
