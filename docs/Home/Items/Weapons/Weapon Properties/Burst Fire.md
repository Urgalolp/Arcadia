A weapon that has the burst fire property can make a normal single-target attack, or it can spray a 10-foot-cube area within range that you can see with shots. Each creature in the area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take the weapon's normal damage. The DC equals 8 + your Dexterity modifier + your proficiency bonus if proficient. This action uses ten pieces of ammunition.

Creatures and objects which are larger than the targeted area automatically fail this saving throw.
