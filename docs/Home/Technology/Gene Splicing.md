Gene splicing is a technological wonder that has been a part of mankind ever since The Null. Through a mixture of alchemy, magic, and some degree of luck, it is possible to alter the physical composition of an individual, introducing and/or removing specific genes within their body. While seemingly miraculous, it is not possible to directly alter one’s genes in order to modify their physical properties, such as height or skin coloration.

Through splicing, it became possible for races to bridge the gap between one another, and the variety of individuals in the world increased dramatically. Furthermore, as it was possible to introduce multiple genes to a fully formed code… within limits, of course… it became possible to alter one’s appearance without severe modifications to their DNA, allowing for the utilization of splicing for aesthetic purposes as well.

Unfortunately, splicing is taxing on the body, and the older an individual, the harder it becomes to appropriately apply this technology to them. Additionally, while this process is groundbreaking, it carries a non-zero mortality rate, which only increases the older the individual or the more complex the splicing.

An individual that has been successfully put through splicing can bear offspring. Such offspring can inherit any splicing present in the bloodline of such an individual, provided the genes are still within the individual, even if in a dormant state.
