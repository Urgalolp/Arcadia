B.R.I.M. is an individual. It lacks any actual name beyond its Acronym, though it is not attached to it sufficiently to call it a name. It lacks a concept of gender, or, to be more precise, lacks a concept of ‘individuality’, and cares little for the idea of being a singular thing.

B.R.I.M. is a warforged created somewhere between 1000 and 1100 A.C., though contrary to most warforged, its precise location of creation, as well as creator, are not identifiable due to the extensive modifications it has performed to its primary body. It is unclear whether or not [[Battlefield Reconnaissance & Intervention Machine|B.R.I.M]]. has simply altered its primary body to such extreme extents, or if it has moved its core into new bodies as time passed.

B.R.I.M. does not support any particular country, nor is supported by any particular country. It refrains from taking actions that would endanger itself, except when the benefit for doing such would outweigh the danger in question. As such, it has gone great lengths to avoid becoming a subject of interest to any country in particular, and often only deals within the underworld.

The few agencies that know of B.R.I.M. know not to provoke it without good reason. B.R.I.M. as an individual is by no means a threat worthy of such, but the extensive time it has been allowed to exist has allowed it to craft a number of artificial lifeforms to serve as extensions of itself, as well as to create weaponry that could cause devastating damage to critical assets involved in removing it from the field. Thus, they willfully ignore it when interaction is unnecessary.
