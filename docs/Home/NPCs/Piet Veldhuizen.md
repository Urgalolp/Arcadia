A Dutch smuggler. Piet’s skills as a smuggler are not incredible… There are others in the market with much more experience and capacity than Piet, as well as those with more resources than her. She runs with a tight crew, tends to use third-parties for many tasks, and often gets in troublesome situations. Of course, Piet’s useful mostly due to availability.

Transport, goods, information, she’s able to get anything not commonly available whenever contacted, sometimes at a premium price, and is always available for contact. Though one certainly would have to wait a while for delivery, and have to put up with her antics, she’s definitively a good choice if the target isn’t something top-secret or top-end.
