Formed in 724, the Office of Extraordinary Affairs, or OEA, was created in the aftermath of The Incursion, with the objective of preventing such events from occurring in the future. The OEA bears the responsibility of acting in the name of mankind itself, and is allowed to bypass the sovereignty of any governmental body if needed.

The OEA has no centralized leadership and, instead, has separated headquarters that each act independently, but all of them follow a specific set of directives that dictate what they should act against. Each member of the OEA is obligated to take a vow and impose upon themselves a seal, as to guarantee their loyalty to the cause over anything else.

Though they have a noble objective, the OEA is not a widespread organization, and their funding is often limited. Few are the countries that know of them, and fewer still are the ones that choose to help. Beyond the low government funding they have, the primary method that the OEA keeps itself afloat is through robbery of supplies, mercenary work, and individual backers of great wealth, though it refrains from any manner of work that would contradict its directives.

With the advancement of technology, the drop in widespread magical knowledge, and the dwindling connection that the world has with other planes, the OEA has been dropping in importance and numbers consistently. In modern times, it often acts more as a counter-terrorism force than much else.
