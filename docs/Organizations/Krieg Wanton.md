The KW are a, presumably, German unit. Outfitted with top-end equipment, designed and bred for combat, Kriegs are units prepared to kill and die for their orders, and are a terror for anything they are deployed against.

Those within the underworld agree that Germany is responsible for the creation, maintenance, and utilization of Kriegs, though no proof has been obtained of such. Surprisingly, the German government does not deny their existence, but denies being involved with them, and actively investigates them.

Amongst all spec-ops units, Kriegs are the only ones known to be used to handle threats and targets with great prejudice, regardless of the cost or sacrifice necessary. They are, effectively, a sledgehammer.
